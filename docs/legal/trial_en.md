# 7‑Day Free Trial (EmoZleep)

Last updated: 2025‑09‑12

## Overview
- New subscribers can try Pro benefits for 7 days for free.
- Even if you cancel during the trial, benefits remain active until the end of day 7.
- If you don’t cancel, your subscription automatically starts after 7 days and you will be charged according to the plan you selected.

## What’s Included During Trial
- Same as Pro benefits:
  - Higher daily conversation limit (vs. Free)
  - Increased preset saves/recommendations
  - Increased task advice
  - Ability to choose conversation models

## Manage/Cancel
- Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
- If you cancel, benefits remain until the end of the trial; then your account reverts to Free.

## Auto‑renewal & Pricing
- If not canceled **at least 24 hours before** the end of the trial, your subscription starts automatically.
- Charges are applied per selected plan: e.g., Pro monthly ₩6,600 / Max monthly ₩11,000 (varies by region/tax).
- Annual plans are charged annually.

## Notes
- Free trial may be offered **once per account**.
- Any **unused portion** of a free trial may be forfeited upon purchase.
- Displayed prices follow StoreKit localization.
